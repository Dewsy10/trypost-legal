# Privacy Policy **Last updated: September 2026** This privacy policy explains how the self-hosted TryPost application handles information when users connect their own social media accounts. The application may process account information and authentication data provided by connected social media platforms. This information is used only to authenticate the user's account and provide the social media management features requested by the user. For connected TikTok accounts, the application may receive basic account information and authorization information required to provide TikTok integration features. The application does not intentionally sell users' personal information. Users control which social media accounts they connect to the application and may disconnect them through the application where supported. Third-party social media platforms may independently collect and process information according to their own privacy policies. Because TryPost is self-hosted, the person or organization operating a particular TryPost installation is responsible for the data stored and processed by that installation. For privacy questions, please contact the application owner through the contact information provided with the application.
