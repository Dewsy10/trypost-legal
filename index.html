TryPost

A self-hosted social media management application for managing and publishing content across connected social media platforms.

What is TryPost?

TryPost is a social media management application that allows users to connect their own social media accounts and manage their content from a single dashboard.

Users can prepare, manage, and publish content to supported social media platforms through their own TryPost installation.

Self-Hosted

TryPost is designed to be self-hosted. This means that the person or organization using TryPost operates their own installation and controls the data stored by that installation.

Users are responsible for the social media accounts they connect, the content they publish, and compliance with the policies of the platforms they use.

Social Media Management

TryPost can be used to connect supported social media platforms, manage media content, and publish authorized content through the respective platform APIs.

Connections to third-party platforms require the user to authenticate and authorize access through those platforms.
